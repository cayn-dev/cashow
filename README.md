Cashow is a personal finance app designed to help people understand where their money goes, stay in control, and build better habits with less friction.

## Why Cashow

Most people do not need more complexity. They need clarity.

Cashow gives users:
- a simple overview of spending and income
- clearer insight into their monthly flow
- visual progress on goals and savings
- AI-powered guidance to identify patterns and opportunities
- a product experience that feels clean, useful, and motivating

## How it works

### 1. Track your finances
Add spending, recurring expenses, salary movements, and investments in one place.

### 2. Understand what changed
See trends, balances, and category performance with a clear dashboard that makes monthly changes easier to read.

### 3. Stay focused on your goals
Create savings targets, monitor progress, and keep momentum through daily financial visibility.

### 4. Ask the app for help
Use AI-powered insights to ask simple questions like:
- How can I save more?
- Where am I overspending?
- What changed this month?

## Key features

- Smart dashboard with balance and spending overview
- AI financial suggestions and insights
- Savings goals with progress tracking
- Subscription and recurring cost monitoring
- Multi-language support
- Privacy-friendly personal finance experience

## Product experience

Cashow combines:
- a clean landing experience for first-time visitors
- a guest mode to try it quickly
- a personal dashboard for active users
- a smooth onboarding flow toward the full product experience
